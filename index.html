  1. My Journey: Where It Started

    My journey into trading began in 2022. When I first discovered it, I saw trading as a way to make quick money. Without proper knowledge, research, or an understanding of the financial markets, I started putting money in, and I experienced losses.

    That experience became a turning point. Instead of giving up, I decided to understand what I had walked into. I began studying the markets, taking educational courses, researching trading concepts, and slowly building a deeper understanding of how trading really works.

  2. Learning Beyond the Markets

    During this period I was also pursuing my BSc in Psychology and Counselling (Honours). That gave me a different lens on human behaviour and decision-making: the role of emotions, fear, anger, patience, self-awareness, and discipline in the choices people make.

    It changed how I looked at trading. Understanding charts and market concepts is only one part of learning. Understanding your own behaviour, emotional responses, and decision-making matters just as much. That connection between market education and psychology became one of the central ideas behind Forex Profit Pathways.

  3. Why I Built Forex Profit Pathways

    I created Forex Profit Pathways so beginners can have structured, understandable, and responsible financial market education. I know what it feels like to start without proper knowledge, and I made mistakes that better preparation could have avoided.

    My goal is to help learners understand the fundamentals, become aware of risk, study trading psychology, and build a more disciplined approach to learning. I do not want anyone to approach trading expecting quick money. I want them to value education, preparation, patience, and responsible decision-making before taking financial risks.

  4. My Vision

    I want to grow Forex Profit Pathways into a comprehensive financial education platform that brings together:

    • Structured educational resources
    • Forex market fundamentals
    • Technical analysis education
    • Risk management awareness
    • Trading psychology and discipline
    • Educational books and courses
    • Interactive learning tools and resources

    The long-term aim is simple: make learning about financial markets more accessible, structured, and responsible.

Good trading education needs two things: knowledge of the markets and discipline over yourself. This platform exists to teach both, honestly and without false promises.
