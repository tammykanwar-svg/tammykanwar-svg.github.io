Risk first

Position sizing starts from how much you can afford to lose on a trade, not from how much you hope to win.

Check with your broker

Contract size, pip definitions, spreads and account currency differ between brokers. Always confirm the numbers in your own platform.

Practice before you risk

Use these tools while studying and practising, so risk management becomes a habit before real money is involved.
