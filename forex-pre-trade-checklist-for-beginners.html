Forex Pre-Trade Checklist for Beginners: 12 Things to Check Before You Enter

By Forex Profit Pathways · Updated 30 October 2026 · 7 min read
Short answer: Before every trade, check the trend, your setup, stop loss, risk reward, lot size, news and your own state of mind. A written checklist stops you from taking impulsive trades, which are the cause of many beginner losses.

Pilots use checklists even after thousands of flights, because skipping one step can be costly. Traders benefit from the same habit. A checklist takes two minutes and filters out many bad trades.

The 12 point checklist

Market and setup

  1. What is the higher timeframe doing? Check the daily or 4 hour chart. Trading with the larger trend is usually easier than trading against it.
  2. Are you at a meaningful level? Look for support, resistance or a clear structure, not the middle of nowhere.
  3. Does the setup match your written plan? If you cannot explain the reason for the trade in one sentence, skip it.
  4. Is there a clear entry trigger? Wait for your signal instead of guessing.

Risk and numbers

  1. Where is the stop loss? Place it where your idea is proven wrong, based on the chart.
  2. Where is the take profit? Use a realistic level from the chart.
  3. Is the risk reward acceptable? Many traders skip trades below 1:1.5 or 1:2. See risk reward ratio explained.
  4. Is the lot size calculated? Risk about 1% or less. Use the Lot Size Calculator or read how to calculate lot size.

Conditions and exposure

  1. Is there major news soon? Check an economic calendar for high impact events such as interest rate decisions, jobs data and inflation reports. Spreads and volatility can jump around them.
  2. Is the spread normal? A wide spread raises your real cost, especially on short stops.
  3. Are you already exposed? Several trades on related pairs (for example EUR/USD and GBP/USD) can act like one big trade. Check your total risk and margin usage.

You

  1. How do you feel? If you are angry, tired, bored or trying to win back a loss, do not trade. This one check can save more money than any indicator.

A short printable version

  • Trend and level are clear
  • Setup matches my plan
  • Stop loss and take profit are set
  • Risk reward meets my minimum
  • Lot size is calculated, risk is 1% or less
  • No major news, normal spread
  • No overlapping trades
  • I am calm and focused

After the trade

A checklist is more powerful when you record the result. Write down the pair, reason, result and any rule you broke in a trade journal. After 30 to 50 trades you will see patterns in your mistakes. Also remember the math of losses in our guide to drawdown recovery.

Common mistakes with checklists

  • Ticking boxes automatically without really checking.
  • Making the list too long so you stop using it. Keep it short.
  • Skipping it "just this once" after a big win or loss. Those are the moments you need it most.

Frequently asked questions

What should I check before entering a forex trade?

Check the higher timeframe trend, key levels, your setup, stop loss, take profit, risk reward, lot size, upcoming news, spread, existing exposure and your emotional state.

How long should a pre-trade checklist take?

About one to two minutes. If it takes much longer, shorten it.

Should I trade during major news?

Many beginners avoid it, since spreads widen and price can move quickly. If you trade news, use smaller size and wider planning.

Do professional traders use checklists?

Many use some form of written rules or a routine to keep decisions consistent and avoid emotional trades.
