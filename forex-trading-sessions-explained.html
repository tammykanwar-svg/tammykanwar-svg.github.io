Forex Trading Sessions Explained: Sydney, Tokyo, London and New York

By Forex Profit Pathways · Updated 30 October 2026 · 7 min read
Short answer: The forex market runs 24 hours a day on weekdays through four sessions: Sydney, Tokyo, London and New York. Activity is usually highest when London and New York overlap, which tends to give tighter spreads and larger moves on major pairs.

Forex has no single exchange. Trading follows the sun around the world, moving from Asia to Europe to North America. Knowing when each session is active helps you pick better times and pairs to trade.

The four sessions (approximate GMT times)

These times shift by about one hour when the US and Europe change to or from daylight saving time, and not on the same dates. Check the hours against your broker's server time and your own time zone.

Sydney session

The week starts here. Liquidity is usually thinner and the market is often calmer. It is more relevant for Australian and New Zealand dollar pairs, and spreads can be wider.

Tokyo session

The main Asian session. Yen pairs are the most active, and many pairs trade in tighter ranges than during Europe or America, though important Asian news can move them.

London session

The largest share of forex trading happens when London is open. Volume and volatility rise at the open, and pairs involving the euro and the pound are most active. Many traders find clear trends and cleaner breakouts here.

New York session

US news such as jobs data, inflation and interest rate decisions are released during this session and can cause quick moves in the US dollar. Activity is strongest in the first hours, then often slows later in the day.

Session overlaps

  • Tokyo and London (about 08:00 to 09:00 GMT): a short overlap with a pickup in activity.
  • London and New York (about 13:00 to 17:00 GMT): usually the busiest time. Liquidity is high, spreads are often tighter, and EUR/USD, GBP/USD and USD/CAD are commonly most active.

More activity also means faster moves, so use a proper stop loss and a sensible lot size. See how to calculate lot size.

Which session should you trade?

  1. Match your schedule. A strategy you can watch carefully is better than a perfect session you cannot attend.
  2. Match your pair. Trade yen pairs in the Asian session and euro or pound pairs in London and New York.
  3. Match your style. Breakout traders often prefer London and the overlap, range traders may prefer quieter hours.
  4. Test it. Record the time of each trade in your trading journal and see when you perform best.

Times to be careful

  • Major news releases, when spreads widen and price can jump. Check an economic calendar.
  • Late New York and the daily rollover, when liquidity is low and spreads can widen.
  • Friday evening, when some traders close positions before the weekend, and weekend gaps are possible.

When is the forex market open?

It opens on Sunday evening GMT (Sydney) and closes on Friday evening GMT (New York). It is closed on weekends, though exact hours depend on your broker.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best time to trade forex?

Many traders prefer the London and New York overlap, roughly 13:00 to 17:00 GMT, because activity is high and spreads are often tighter. The best time still depends on your pair, strategy and schedule.

Is forex open 24 hours?

It is open 24 hours a day from Sunday evening to Friday evening GMT, and closed over the weekend.

Which currency pairs move most in the Asian session?

Yen pairs such as USD/JPY and AUD/JPY, plus Australian and New Zealand dollar pairs, are typically the most active.

Do session times change during the year?

Yes. Daylight saving changes in the US and Europe shift local session times by about an hour, so check them twice a year.
