How it works

The tool plays your numbers 500 times using random wins and losses. Each trade risks the chosen % of the current balance and wins or loses it by the reward ratio. Costs, slippage and changing win rates are not included.

This is a simulation, not a forecast. Real results can be worse.

Educational tool only. Trading forex involves substantial risk and no outcome is guaranteed. This is not financial advice.
