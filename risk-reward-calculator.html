How it works

Risk : reward = distance to take profit ÷ distance to stop loss. Breakeven win rate = 1 ÷ (1 + ratio). Expectancy (in R) = win rate × ratio − (1 − win rate). One R is the amount you risk on the trade.

Expectancy is an average over many trades, not a promise for the next one.
